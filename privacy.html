MUST Finals - Privacy Policy

MUST Finals (must-finals.github.io) is a free study platform for MUST cybersecurity students, run by Mezo Taher. Contact: mezotaher61@gmail.com.

Services This Site Uses

  • GitHub Pages serves the site to your browser.
  • Google handles sign-in. The site only asks for your name, email address and profile picture (the standard openid, email and profile scopes). The site never sees your Google password, Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Supabase hosts the database (accounts, study progress, discussion posts) and runs sign-in.
  • GoatCounter provides visit statistics. Per GoatCounter's own privacy policy, it sets no cookies, does not store IP addresses, and shows only aggregate counts (page views, browser type, country). Source: goatcounter.com/help/privacy

We do not sell or rent your data, and the site runs no ads or marketing trackers.

If You Sign In

  • At sign-in, Google sends your name, email address and profile picture to the sign-in system (Supabase Auth), which keeps them in its private account store. That store is visible to the site owner, not to other students.
  • Your account also has a display name you can edit.
  • Your study progress and quiz results are stored so they sync across your devices. They are not visible to other students. (The site owner and the database provider technically host all stored data.)
  • Signed-in students can read student profile names used in discussions. The site owner's admin profile is hidden from student profile reads; only the owner can open the private admin dashboard. Do not put private information in your display name.
  • Your email address and study progress are not visible to other students.
  • Discussion posts are visible to all signed-in students.
  • Questions and answers in the Student Help Center are visible to signed-in students and show your chosen profile name. Your email and account ID are not shown in the interface.
  • Help Center reports are visible only to the site owner/admin. Campus guide entries are curated by the site owner with a source link and review date; they are not official university announcements.
  • The account menu and admin dashboard are visible only after sign-in, and the dashboard entry is shown only to the configured site owner. Students can contact the owner at mezotaher61@gmail.com; this support address is intentionally public.
  • If you report a problem with a question, your report text is visible only to you and the admin.

You must sign in or create an account before entering the study workspace. The login screen controls access through the app; because the site is hosted as a static public website, published course files and page source may still be reachable directly by URL.

AI Study Features

  • The Smart Study Planner works locally with your study preferences and course progress. Its optional AI Study Coach requires a signed-in account and active server-side configuration.
  • Only when you choose “Review my plan with AI,” the site sends a limited study summary to the MUST Finals server and Groq: subject progress, practice accuracy, exam and assignment dates, task titles, availability, and busy study times. Your name, email, and account ID are not sent to the model. Avoid putting personal details in task titles.
  • The platform stores daily request counts for per-account and shared limits. It does not store prompts or answers. Groq processes the request under its service terms and account data controls.
  • AI suggestions are unofficial and may be wrong. Review generated sessions before adding them to your calendar. The AI Tutor, PDF chat, and AI Quiz Generator remain dependent on their separate provider configuration and access controls.

Your Requests

You can email mezotaher61@gmail.com to ask what is stored about you or to ask for your account and saved progress to be deleted.

Changes

If how the site handles data changes, this page will be updated first.
