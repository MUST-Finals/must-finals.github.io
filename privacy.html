MUST Finals - Privacy Policy

MUST Finals (must-finals.github.io) is a free study platform for MUST cybersecurity students, run by Mezo Taher. Contact: mezotaher61@gmail.com.

Services This Site Uses

  • GitHub Pages serves the site to your browser.
  • Google handles sign-in. The site only asks for your name, email address and profile picture (the standard openid, email and profile scopes). The site never sees your Google password, Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Supabase hosts the database (accounts, study progress, discussion posts) and runs sign-in.
  • GoatCounter provides visit statistics. Per GoatCounter's own privacy policy, it sets no cookies, does not store IP addresses, and shows only aggregate counts (page views, browser type, country). Source: goatcounter.com/help/privacy

We do not sell or rent your data, and the site runs no ads or marketing trackers.

If You Sign In

  • At sign-in, Google sends your name, email address and profile picture to the sign-in system (Supabase Auth), which keeps them in its private account store. That store is visible to the site owner, not to other students.
  • Your account also has a display name you can edit.
  • Your study progress and quiz results are stored so they sync across your devices. They are not visible to other students. (The site owner and the database provider technically host all stored data.)
  • Other signed-in students can read every user's profile: display name, role (student, admin or blocked), account creation date and user ID. This is how discussion posts show who wrote them. Do not put private information in your display name.
  • Your email address and study progress are not visible to other students.
  • Discussion posts are visible to all signed-in students.
  • If you report a problem with a question, your report text is visible only to you and the admin.

You can use most of the site without signing in.

AI TUTOR - currently off

  • If an AI tutor ever launches, this policy will be updated first with exactly how it handles data. No AI feature is active now, and no study data is sent to any AI service.

Your Requests

You can email mezotaher61@gmail.com to ask what is stored about you or to ask for your account and saved progress to be deleted.

Changes

If how the site handles data changes, this page will be updated first.
