Privacy Policy - MUST Finals

Last updated: November 21, 2026

MUST Finals (mazentaher.github.io/must-finals) is a free study platform for MUST cybersecurity students, built and run by Mezo Taher.

What data is collected

  • If you choose to sign in, we receive your Google account's basic profile information only: your name, email address, and profile picture (the standard openid, email and profile scopes). Sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase Auth; we never see or store your Google password.
  • Study progress and quiz results you save while signed in, stored in our Supabase database so your progress syncs across devices.
  • Anonymous, aggregate page-view statistics via GoatCounter. No cookies, no personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not read your Gmail, Drive, or any other Google data beyond the basic profile fields above.
  • We do not use advertising or marketing trackers.

Where data is stored

The site is served from GitHub Pages. Account and progress data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) project controlled by the site owner.

Your choices

  • You can use most of the site without signing in.
  • You can ask for your account and saved progress to be deleted at any time by emailing mezotaher61@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy: mezotaher61@gmail.com.
